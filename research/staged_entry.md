# Staged entry: all at once or in monthly tranches, while drawing income

Compares investing the starting cash all at once (lump sum) with moving it in equal monthly tranches, while drawing the fixed CPI-indexed income, per core, staging period, cash variant and withdrawal rate. A comparison only: no pass/fail rule and no search trial; it selects nothing and recommends nothing. Spec: docs/methodology/staged-entry.md.

- data: up to 2018-12-31 only (validation and holdout stay sealed)
- staged_entry.yaml sha256: `b56741d1d0c643babfaded68bea3a8fd12bb50e38bef1381b74e73d95aa52273`
- decision.yaml sha256: `88ab8b08a34f8b3d106fd7670af02e3e827b86467a2755eb3620ada4490aa76d` (read unchanged)
- longrun.yaml sha256: `ebd3d59afd1bfbc79118747a944f74a8b3c62688aeddfe2e201183e10acfe061` (read unchanged)
- historical view: 120 starts, the first trading day of each month 2005-01 to 2014-12, each to 2018-12-31; primary measure at 48 months (the start month counts as the first)
- long run: 10000 paths of 30 years, 12-month blocks drawn from 168 months (2005-01 to 2018-12, circular), seed 20261008; the same paths as the long-run report
- trading costs: 5 bps per unit traded
- entry: lump sum, 6 months, 12 months, 24 months
- cash variants: SHY = waiting cash in SHY (actual prices); CPI+1% = waiting cash at inflation + 1% real (assumption)
- portfolios: A (current: QQQ/GLD 200d trend); B (SPY/GLD 200d trend); C (SPY/IEF 200d trend); D (static 60/40 SPY/IEF); E (static SPY 60 / IEF 20 / GLD 20); F (switched mix, SPY 200d signal); G (static 25% each SPY/TLT/GLD/SHY)
- 7 portfolios x 4 entries x 4 withdrawal rates = 112 rows per view, one column group per cash variant

## Notes

- A comparison: it selects nothing and recommends nothing.
- The 120 historical starts overlap heavily (each 48-month window shares most of its months with the next), so they are not 120 independent tests.
- Withdrawals (fixed, CPI-indexed) come from waiting cash first, then from the invested part, proportionally across the tranches already bought. On a day with both, the withdrawal comes first, then the tranche, then the day's returns.
- Waiting cash at inflation + 1% real is an assumption, not a fund. SHY's yields were near zero in 2009-2015, so the SHY variant understates today's cash rates.
- Each historical tranche is a fresh purchase paying 5 bps on entry; the long run charges none, which favours staging by at most 5 bps of the starting value. The SHY cash leg is the CASH portfolio held from the first research day, so only the first start's cash pays its 5 bps entry.
- Gap: staged minus lump-sum real value, in percentage points of the starting value. Above lump: share of starts where staging ended strictly above. Max drawdown: of the total value (cash plus tranches, after withdrawals) from the start to 48 months, the starting value counting as a peak. Worst drawdown vs lump: the largest per-start staged-minus-lump difference.
- Flexible withdrawal rules together with staging are deferred.
- The historical period had a long rising market, which favours the lump sum.
